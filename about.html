About Us

Tamu Express connects buyers with supermarkets, retailers, and wholesalers, then coordinates the delivery journey.

The platform is built to make discovery, ordering, and movement easier. Buyers browse trusted sellers in one place, businesses join through approval, and Tamu Express stays in the middle as the coordinating layer.

What We Do

We connect demand and supply.

Buyers find products, sellers list offers, and Tamu Express helps the order move in a structured way.

Our Role

We act as the intermediary.

That means visibility, coordination, and delivery support stay under one marketplace system.

Who We Serve

Buyers, supermarkets, retailers, and wholesalers.

The platform supports both normal household buying and larger supply-focused ordering needs.
